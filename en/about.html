Flepness & other things

17 January 2020

Flepness Paradise

In January 2020, it was time for Fleptor to receive ownership of DC for a week. Fleptor had an idea to do something special for his week by turning the place into a “Fleptorized” version of DC, and he’d call it “Flepness Paradise”.

It didn’t take Fleptor long to figure out that after his ownership was done, all the effort would be reset, so he decided to turn it into an actual Discord server. On 17 January 2020, Flepness Paradise was created.

History & updates

Flepness Broadcast

Formerly known as Flepness Paradise Broadcast, Flepness Broadcast is a show founded and hosted by Fleptor and uploaded to YouTube. The show mainly talks about Discord servers which Fleptor had a role in, their stories, members, and history.

The plan was to make 14 episodes. Six were made, and I paused to focus on education.

22 June 2022

Flepness Paradise ARG

The Alternate Reality Game of Flepness Paradise revolves around the server, its owner, and the owner’s relationship with himself, his friends, and the community.

On the afternoon of 22 June 2022, Mohammad Bassam noticed the disappearance of all Flepness Paradise channels after receiving an encrypted message from Fleptor. Fleptor’s friends embarked on a series of puzzles, moving between Discord servers, YouTube, Messenger, and Twitter. The story ended with a Minecraft duel.

5 Basics of Building in Minecraft

Due to the lack of educational Minecraft content on Arabic YouTube, and after looking for an alternative, I decided to make a video explaining what I believe to be the five basics of building in Minecraft: depth, block palette, scale, shape, and planning.

Fun fact: this video was one of my motives to continue working on Flepness Broadcast.
