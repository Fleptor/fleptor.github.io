Flepsite

About this website

A personal website made by Fleptor for Fleptor: a platform for my personal works, blog posts, and life plans. It began as an attempt to learn how to make a static website, and I’m still working on it.

Source on GitHubHomepageWhy I made Flepsite (Arabic)

The first attempt — 2022

Before the space wallpaper and the Flepsite name, this was my first attempt at a static website. It already had the main pages: Home, About, Blog, and fp.

Flepsite — August 2022

The Flepsite name, space wallpaper, and animated title were here. The site had pages for my projects and Flepness Broadcast episodes, in Arabic and English.

The homepage update — August 2024

In the summer 2024 update, I rearranged the homepage into panels for recent additions, the blog, and my works. The wallpaper, fonts, and blue colours stayed.

The homepage today — October 2026

The homepage now brings together my websites, projects, and a few moments from my life. It is still Flepsite, with an updated homepage and a works page for the project details.
